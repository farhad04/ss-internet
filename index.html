Self help

Router & WiFi Help

Quick troubleshooting tips before contacting support.

📶 Slow WiFi

Place the router in an open, central location; avoid hiding it inside cabinets.

🔄 Restart Router

Power off the router for about 20 seconds, then turn it back on and wait for it to reconnect.

🔐 WiFi Password

Change your WiFi password through your router settings. If you need help, contact our support team.

🛠️ Still not working?

Send your name, address and a short description of the problem to support.
